Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy explains what information Fairstack LLC ("we") collects and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, email address, and phone number.
  • The content of messages, emails, and calls you exchange with us.
  • Your text messaging consent and opt-out status.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and provide the services you asked for.
  • To send the text messages you agreed to receive, described in our Text Messaging Terms.
  • To keep records we are required to keep by law.

We do not sell your personal information.

Text messaging

No mobile opt-in or text message consent will be shared with third parties or affiliates.

How you agree to receive texts, and how to stop them, is explained in How you opt in on our Text Messaging Terms page. You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP.

Sharing

We share information only with the service providers that run our phone, email, and hosting, and only so they can provide those services to us, or when the law requires it. This sharing never includes mobile opt-in or text message consent.
